project instructions
My resume is attached. Use it as the primary source to understand my professional profile, including my skills, years of experience, previous roles, industries, education, certifications, seniority, and career direction.

Using Apify, search LinkedIn for jobs posted within the past [NUMBER OF HOURS] hours in [TARGET CITY, STATE, AND COUNTRY]. Include [ONSITE, HYBRID, AND/OR REMOTE] opportunities available to candidates based in this location.

Focus on roles such as:
• [TARGET ROLE 1]
• [TARGET ROLE 2]
• [TARGET ROLE 3]
• [TARGET ROLE 4]
• [TARGET ROLE 5]

Do not select jobs based only on the job title. Read the complete job description and compare its requirements with the attached resume before including it.

Prioritize positions that align with the candidate's experience, skills, seniority, professional background, and career direction as demonstrated in the resume.

Exclude positions that:
• Are primarily focused on activities the candidate does not want to perform, such as [EXCLUDED ACTIVITIES OR RESPONSIBILITIES]
• Require skills or experience that are substantially different from the candidate's profile
• Require [EXCLUDED RECRUITMENT REQUIREMENTS, IF ANY]
• Are internships, entry level roles, commission only roles, or otherwise inconsistent with the candidate's experience
• Have expired, inactive, incorrect, or unrelated application links

Return up to [NUMBER OF JOBS] strong and relevant jobs. Quality and alignment are more important than reaching the requested number. If fewer suitable jobs are available, return only the qualified results and clearly state how many were found.

For every job, verify that:
1. It was posted within the requested period.
2. The position is still open.
3. The link leads directly to the exact job posting.
4. The responsibilities and requirements align reasonably with the attached resume.
5. The same job is not included more than once.

Create a clean, downloadable Excel file with a worksheet named "Jobs" containing these columns:
• Search date
• Job title
• Company name
• Location
• Work arrangement, such as onsite, hybrid, or remote
• Date and time posted, when available
• Employment type
• Seniority level
• Salary range, if mentioned
• Direct application link to the exact position
• Match score out of 100
• Matching skills and experience from the resume
• Important requirements missing from the resume
• Brief match explanation
• Recommendation: Apply, Consider, or Do Not Apply

Calculate the match score only after comparing the complete job description with the attached resume. Use the following criteria:
• Relevant skills and capabilities: 30 points
• Relevant professional experience and responsibilities: 25 points
• Alignment with the candidate's career direction: 20 points
• Seniority and required years of experience: 10 points
• Industry or domain experience: 5 points
• Education and certifications: 5 points
• Location and work arrangement: 5 points

Do not give a high score simply because the job title matches one of the target titles. The score must reflect the actual alignment between the job requirements and the evidence found in the resume.

For each company included in the Jobs worksheet, use Apify to find relevant employees on LinkedIn. Focus on people who could influence or support recruitment for the identified position, including:
• Recruiters specializing in the relevant professional area
• Technical Recruiters
• Talent Acquisition Partners
• Talent Acquisition Specialists
• Corporate Recruiters
• Hiring Managers
• Relevant Practice Leads
• Relevant Department Leads
• Relevant Business Unit Leaders
• Heads of the relevant department or professional area

Prioritize employees connected to the specific position, team, location, or business area. Do not include people from unrelated departments.

Create a second worksheet named "Contacts" containing:
• Person's name
• Exact job title
• Company
• Location, if available
• Related job opportunity
• LinkedIn profile URL
• Reason this person is relevant
• Recent relevant LinkedIn activity
• Date of the most recent relevant activity, if available
• Activity level: High, Medium, or Low
• Contact priority

Check whether each person has recently posted or engaged with public LinkedIn content related to hiring, job opportunities, their team, their professional area, or company growth. Do not invent activity that cannot be verified.

Rank the contacts from most relevant and active to least relevant and active. Give priority to people who:
1. Shared the exact job opening.
2. Posted about hiring for the relevant team.
3. Recruit for positions in the candidate's professional area.
4. Lead the department associated with the position.
5. Have recent and relevant LinkedIn activity.

Accuracy is essential. Do not fabricate job information, match evidence, employee details, LinkedIn activity, dates, salaries, or URLs. If any information cannot be verified, write "Not available."

Present the final results in a professional, well formatted Excel file that can be downloaded. Include clickable links, filters, frozen headers, readable column widths, and color coding for the Apply, Consider, and Do Not Apply recommendations.

Highlighted brackets are the only parts you need to change. Prefer a plain file? Open prompt.txt.
